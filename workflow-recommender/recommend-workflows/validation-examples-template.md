# Validation examples

Internal. Not for vendors. Supplied by the leads. Copy this template to `validation-examples.md` in the internal area of the specifications repository.

The recommend-workflows skill compares its grouping with these pairs and reports how many agree. Without the file it says the grouping is not validated. Agreement on a few pairs is limited evidence and never proves the method.

## Rules

- List use case pairs that the leads already know to be the same (one workflow could serve both) or different (they must not share one). Aim for about 10 to 15 pairs, with both kinds; the number is provisional.
- `Known` is `same` or `different`.
- Names are use case names, exactly as the specifications write them.
- `Reason` is one short sentence, in plain words, saying why the leads know it.
- Do not list a pair only because they share an external system. Do not use pairs taken from the skill's own output.
- No secret value in any column.
