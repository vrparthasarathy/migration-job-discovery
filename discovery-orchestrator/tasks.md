## Requirement

A coordinating GitHub Copilot skill: a lead chooses a use-case folder and the skill runs the discovery stages in sequence (source screening, identity and access, behavior documentation, workflow recommendation), runs the review CSV exporter, walks the lead through the pending rows in the flow itself (conflicts, suggestions, then plain adds, with accept-all of one use case's plain adds), and processes the next folder without duplicates. It proposes and records the lead's own decisions only and never writes to the tracker. Depends on the Sample Tracker Workbook being built and its format reviewed by the team; the plan is a first draft to refine after that feedback. Excludes the sample workbook, the infrastructure scope and the `same as` shortcut.
